How the course runs

  1. 7 April

    Online introductionAn introduction on Zoom to the course material, so that every student starts the course with the knowledge it assumes, and the first module: the principles and the ways of thinking needed to understand, analyse, design and develop exercise, including the science of force and its relation to exercise.

  2. 16 to 18 April

    Three days in personPractical workshops that recap and explore the principles from the online content: applying them to the analysis and design of exercises, and using cues to improve execution.

  3. 19 April

    Online theoryThe last three modules: the anatomy of the spine and trunk and its exercises; the lower body and its exercises; the upper body and its exercises.

  4. 12 May

    Live video reviewA group question-and-answer session, so that everyone has understood the modules before the exam.

  5. 14 to 23 May

    The examTo be certified as an RTS Level 1 Practitioner you pass the RTS 1 exam: a video submission and two multiple-choice exams, the first with a video clip for each question.
